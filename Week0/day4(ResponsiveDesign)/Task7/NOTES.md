## Steps to Enable GitHub Pages:
1. Go to repository **Settings** on GitHub.
2. Click on **Pages** in the left sidebar.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or your active branch) and root `/`.
4. Click **Save**.
5. The live site becomes available in a couple of minutes.
