Classic Tomato Basil Pasta

A quick, fresh, and delicious weeknight dinner made with simple pantry ingredients and fresh basil.

Recipe Information

Prep and Cook Time

Preparation: 10 mins | Cooking: 15 mins | Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 200g dried spaghetti or penne pasta
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 400g canned chopped Italian tomatoes
  • 1 pinch dried red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Fresh basil leaves, torn
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for serving

Instructions

  1. Boil salted water and cook pasta until al dente.
  2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Add garlic and pepper flakes, cooking gently for 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Drain pasta (reserve 2 tbsp of pasta water).
  6. Toss pasta in the skillet with sauce and reserved water.
  7. Stir in fresh basil, top with Parmesan, and serve.
